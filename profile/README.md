# Data Bridge Consulting

**From data to decisions.**

Data Bridge connects complex data, advanced AI and existing enterprise workflows to produce
traceable decision support inside clear governance and accountability boundaries.

## What we build

- **Governed AI research** — analysis pipelines where every number is traceable to its source,
  and the reasoning is auditable rather than assumed.
- **Decision-support workflows** — AI that fits existing enterprise processes and
  accountability lines, not around them.

## Projects

- **Aquara Lab** — a governed AI research engine for traceable, auditable analysis.
- **Data Bridge Consulting** — decision-support workflows connecting data, AI and
  organisational accountability.
